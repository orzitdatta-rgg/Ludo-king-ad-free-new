      How to play
      • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its yard.
      • Land on a lone opponent token to send it back home. Stars and start squares are safe.
      • A 6, a capture, or getting a token home earns another roll.
      • Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
      • You need the exact number to reach the center.
      • First player to bring all four tokens to the center wins.

      No ads and no accounts. Games run between the players' devices.
